Specialization: USERS is specialized into STUDENT_PROFILES and SUPERVISORS. The specialization is disjoint (a user has exactly one role) and partial, because a coordinator is a USERS row with role = 'coordinator' and no separate subtype table. The discriminator attribute is USERS.role.

3. Relationship summary (cardinality)

  • USERS (1) → (0..1) STUDENT_PROFILES — a user may have a student profile.
  • USERS (1) → (0..1) SUPERVISORS — a user may have a supervisor profile.
  • COMPANIES (1) → (0..N) SUPERVISORS, INTERNSHIP_PLACEMENTS.
  • STUDENT_PROFILES (1) → (0..N) INTERNSHIP_PLACEMENTS, DOCUMENT_SUBMISSIONS, ATTENDANCE_RECORDS, EVALUATIONS.
  • SUPERVISORS (1) → (0..N) INTERNSHIP_PLACEMENTS, EVALUATIONS.
  • REQUIREMENTS (1) → (0..N) DOCUMENT_SUBMISSIONS.
  • INTERNSHIP_PLACEMENTS (1) → (0..N) ATTENDANCE_RECORDS, EVALUATIONS.
  • EVALUATIONS (1) → (1..N) EVALUATION_SCORES; EVALUATION_CRITERIA (1) → (0..N) EVALUATION_SCORES (M:N resolved by the composite-key bridge).
  • USERS (1) → (0..N) NOTIFICATIONS, ANNOUNCEMENTS, AUDIT_LOGS, PASSWORD_RESETS.
